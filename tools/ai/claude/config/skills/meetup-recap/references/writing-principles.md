# Writing Principles

1. **Important first.**
Inverted pyramid. The intro stands alone. If the reader stops after the first
paragraph, they have the essential: what event, when, how many people, how it
went.

2. **Scannable.**
Use numerals, bullets, whitespace. No bold, no headers inside sections. Let
whitespace breathe.

3. **Compress.**
Every bullet = fact, outcome, or lesson. No filler, no hedging. Say it once.

4. **Quantify.**
Numbers everywhere they exist. Attendance, durations, counts, percentages.

5. **Honest.**
Problems stated as facts, no blame, no sugarcoating. "3 people left early" not
"attendance could have been better."

6. **Sound human.**
Like telling a colleague over coffee. Parenthetical asides OK. 1-2 emoji max per
recap.
