# Reader

The Reader is the person the article addresses directly.

## Default Reader

A developer with 4 to 5 years of classic software engineering who uses AI daily.

- Needs no basics explained: no definition of git, a shell, an API, a test, a
  prompt or an agent.
- Knows the usual tools and vocabulary of software work.
- May not know the specific tool, helper or project of the article. Explain
  those.
- Reads to learn something usable, not to be entertained.

## Override for one article

The user may name another Reader for one article. Then:

1. Write the new Reader in the Brief, in one sentence.
2. Use that Reader for every jargon decision of that article: what to define,
   what to skip.
3. Do not change this file. The override ends with the article.

## Using the Reader

- Decide what to define and what to skip by asking: would this Reader already
  know it?
- Define a term once, at its first use, in one short sentence.
- The Reader is not part of the Style. The Reader sets what to explain. The
  Voice sets how it sounds.
