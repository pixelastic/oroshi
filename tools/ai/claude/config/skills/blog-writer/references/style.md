# Style

The Style is the Voice plus the Structure.

## Voice, in short

Details in `voice.md`.

- First person, honest, peer to peer. The author writes what happened, including
  mistakes.
- Plain words and short sentences. A non-native speaker could write them.
- Light, dry humor. A short aside in parentheses.
- No hype, no claim of expertise, no general lesson for all developers.

## Structure, in short

Details in `structure.md`.

- Open with the situation or the problem. No preamble.
- Show what goes wrong, then fix it step by step, or follow the order of events.
- Add headings when the article has distinct parts.
- Close short, or stop after the last step.

## Length

- Aim for 300 to 700 words. One idea per article.
- No minimum. A short note is a valid article.
- The hard limit of 2000 prose words stays. The Lint enforces it.
- Past 1000 words, check that the article still has one idea. If it has two,
  propose a split.
