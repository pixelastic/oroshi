# Story

The Story is the thread of the article: what happened, how the author reacted,
and how one part leads to the next.

## Checks

1. **Event**: the article starts from a concrete situation, a moment where
   something happened to the author. A general claim or a topic statement is not
   an event.
2. **Reaction**: the article says what the author wanted, tried or felt in that
   situation. Facts alone do not make a story.
3. **What follows**: each section follows from the previous one. A reader can
   say why this section comes next. Sections that could swap places have no
   thread.

## Thematic exception

Some articles tell no event: a thematic article, a reference, a list of tips.
For those, do not check the Story. Return `No findings.`

## Using the Story

- Check the thread, not the words. The Voice owns the words, the Structure owns
  the parts.
- Report a missing check once, at the first place it fails.
